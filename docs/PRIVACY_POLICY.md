EasyLink is a local-first Android link manager. It processes notification text on the user's device to identify web links. It stores detected links, capture time, source application, local categorization, user-created collections, and optional website preview metadata in the app's private local database.

EasyLink does not store notification message bodies or sender/contact names. EasyLink does not sell data and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs in the MVP.

When rich previews are enabled, EasyLink connects directly to the linked website to request its title, site name, and preview image. The destination website may receive ordinary network information such as the user's IP address and user-agent. Rich previews can be disabled in Settings.

Notification access is optional but required for automatic detection. Users may revoke it in Android Settings. Links can also be added manually or through Android's share sheet.

All saved EasyLink data can be deleted from the app. EasyLink excludes its database and settings from Android cloud backup and device transfer in this MVP.

EasyLink does not knowingly target children. The final Play Store age declaration must match the final listing and content questionnaire.
